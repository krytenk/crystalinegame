Crystalline Privacy Policy

Departure Bay Digital · Effective date: 1 August 2026 · Product: Crystalline (package ca.departurebaydigital.crystalline)

This policy describes how the Crystalline mobile app handles information. It is written for Google Play disclosure requirements.

Summary

Crystalline is designed to run primarily on your device. Progress, settings, and economy state are stored locally on the phone. We do not operate a Crystalline account system and we do not sell personal data.

Data the app stores on your device

  • Game progress (levels cleared, stars, cavern furnishings)
  • Settings (sound, haptics, accessibility options)
  • Local economy state (lives, essence, simulated shop flags)

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app deletes this data. There is no cloud restore unless a future update adds one (we will update this policy first).

Network use

The app may use the internet to load optional demo video content (for example YouTube embeds used as placeholder “ad” creatives in research builds). That traffic is handled by the third-party video provider under their own policies.

Core match-3 play works offline once the app is installed.

Purchases & ads

Current builds treat the shop and ad placements as simulated for portfolio / research purposes. No real money is charged by Crystalline unless a future update integrates Google Play Billing or a certified ad SDK. If that happens, this policy will be revised and the store listing will reflect real transactions.

Permissions

  • Internet — optional video embeds / future services
  • Vibrate — haptic feedback during play (can be disabled in settings)

Children

Crystalline is a casual puzzle game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can disable network access for the app in system settings if desired.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product ships store features. The effective date at the top will change when we do.
