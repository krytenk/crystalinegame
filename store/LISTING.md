Descend into the Crystal Cavern.

Crystalline is a portrait match-3 about light, pressure, and pretty explosions. Swap gems to forge power crystals, drop golden artifacts, crack crust, and defuse bombs — then spend essence furnishing a living cavern.

FEATURES
• Classic match-3 with cascades, power gems, and a victory sugar-crush
• Shaped boards and chamber bosses with multi-goal challenges
• Crystal Cavern meta: clear levels, earn essence, place furnishings
• Daily dives, album shards, and soft live-ops events
• Comfort options: haptics, soft hints, accessibility glyphs

HOW TO PLAY
1. Swap adjacent gems to make lines of 3 or more
2. Match 4+ to forge Seam Rifts, Geode Bursts, and rarer specials
3. Complete every goal before moves run out
4. Watch leftover moves explode into a victory cascade
5. Spend essence to grow your cavern between dives

Boss chambers hit harder with stacked goals — but gems stay big and readable on phones.
