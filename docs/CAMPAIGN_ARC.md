# Campaign & story arc plan — Crystalline · Lantern Harbor

**Status:** Planning (2026-08-03)  
**Products:** Shared level catalogue; per-theme chapter names, companion copy, meta stages.  
**Engine:** No new core systems required for volume; prefer content + layout + objective pressure.

---

## 1. North star

| Phase | Levels | Role | Ship when |
|-------|--------|------|-----------|
| **Launch spine** | **1–150** | Existing catalogue (Mouth → Regent Peak / Pier → Apex) | **Now** (shipped) |
| **Launch complete** | **151–300** | Full **Act I** story + map depth for store launch | **Before public launch** |
| **Phase 2 (future)** | **301–600** | **Act II** story arc — sequel campaign / “season 2” | **Post-launch** content pillars |
| **Beyond** | 601+ | Act III / prestige / endless chapters | Only after 301–600 proves retention |

**Product promise at launch:** *300 levels, one complete mine/harbor story, dual-loop meta still has room to grow.*  
**Future-proof promise:** *301–600 is designed as a second act, not random more levels.*

Harbor and Crystalline **share mechanical levels** (same JSON). Story/chapter names diverge by theme pack.

### Signature pillars (identity — do not trade these for novel board toys)

Players will call this “another match-3” if only the *swap grammar* is memorable. Protect what makes the studio products *place-first*:

| Pillar | What it is | Guardrail |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| **Place that grows** | Dual-loop meta: caverns / docks, stages, props, idle sinks | Prefer stage drops & ceremony over pure currency dumps |
| **One engine, two myths** | Shared levels; Crystalline + Harbor voice, art, map, companion | Never ship a theme that is only a palette swap |
| **Story-shaped campaign** | Acts, chapters, finales — mountain/tide that answers back | Act II is a second act, not “harder random levels” |
| **Legible match grammar** | Familiar swap / cascade / blockers so layout & goals can shine | Invent control rules only when story *earns* them (e.g. locking crust ~361+) |
| **Material brand** | Kinetic / tactile wordmarks and UI that feel mine or harbor | Signature lives in ritual & touch, not in obscure mechanics |
| **Honest live product** | Fair difficulty grammar, ethical F2P literacy, real “what’s new” | Don’t solve identity by stacking opaque systems |

**Design test:** Would a player describe the game as *“another match-3”* or as *“the mine / harbor that grows when I play”?* If the second answer is true, do not overcomplicate the board to prove originality.

### How far ahead to plan (realistic horizon)

| Horizon | Plan depth | Examples |
|---------|------------|----------|
| **Now → launch (Act I)** | **Full build plan** | Levels 151–300, LEVEL_PASS, chapters, meta stages 5–8, ship gates |
| **Post-launch 0–6 months** | **Schedule + pillars** | Monthly level packs, quarterly docks/cavern, Bakery/Library stagger (see `docs/STUDIO_FOCUS.md`) |
| **Act II (301–600)** | **Synopsis + gates only** | Loglines, chapter titles, crust-lock band, boss fantasies, meta stage names — **not** full level JSON |

**Rule of thumb:** *Write the map so you don’t paint yourself into a corner; only build the road under your feet.* Over-planning level content past the next act burns energy that should go into shipping and learning from real play.

## 2. Story architecture (both products, parallel myth)

Think in **acts**, not only chapters. Each act has a **fantasy verb**, **blocker mix**, and a **finale boss band**.

### Act I — Launch (levels 1–300)

**Through-line**

| Product | Act I logline |
|---------|----------------|
| **Crystalline** | Descend the living mountain: from the Mouth to the Regent Peak, then through the **Under-Crown** where the mountain starts *answering back*. |
| **Lantern Harbor** | Restore the docks from Pier Lights to Harbor Apex, then push into the **Outer Channels** where the tide and rival lanterns rewrite the night. |

**Three movements inside Act I**

| Movement | Levels | Story beat | Play feel |
|----------|--------|------------|-----------|
| **I-A Arrival** | 1–100 | Learn the loop; forge powers; first bosses; meta stage 1–3 | Tutorial → mid multi-threat |
| **I-B Depth** | 101–150 | Apex of “classic” mine/harbor; full toolset; stages 3–4 | Catalogue polish (existing) |
| **I-C Under-Crown / Outer Channels** | **151–300** | **New region.** Story turns: the mountain/tide is not passive. Dual threats permanent. | Launch expansion (to build) |

**Act I finale (L291–300):** stacked multi-objective “regent” bosses + short epilogue clears so the player *feels finished* but the map still shows locked Phase 2.

### Act II — Phase 2 story (levels 301–600)

**Through-line**

| Product | Act II logline |
|---------|----------------|
| **Crystalline** | **The Fracture** — a fault splits the crown; light from the surface mixes with deep void; the Living Core may not be alone. |
| **Lantern Harbor** | **Storm Treaty** — fog fleets, sealed buoys, a lighthouse that answers a second signal; festival light becomes diplomacy under weather. |

**Three movements inside Act II**

| Movement | Levels | Story beat | Play feel |
|----------|--------|------------|-----------|
| **II-A Fracture opens** | 301–400 | New region identity; re-introduce tools under new constraints | Fresh map art / chamber tints |
| **II-B Rival systems** | 401–500 | Peak density multi-obj; “two pressures at once” as fantasy | Harder walls, fair fuses |
| **II-C Crown / Treaty** | 501–600 | Arc finale; optional prestige bosses; leave door to Act III | Ceremony + meta stage drops |

**Act II is not required for launch.** Design it now so map roman numerals, companion lines, and meta stages do not paint us into a corner.

## 4. Difficulty & content grammar (tighter going forward)

Lessons from L26 pure-contain and L23 screen size apply to **all new bands**.

### Hard rules for L151–600

1. **No pure free-win single objectives** after L25-style bosses (especially pure contain / pure score spikes without layout pressure).  
2. **Boss every 10** is multi-threat (≥2 objective kinds) from L160 onward.  
3. **Shadow period:** prefer **2** when contain is present in mid/late bands; avoid period 3 free spreads.  
4. **Fuse safety:** `moves >= bombFuse`; multi-bomb → fuse ≥ 4; moves ≥ fuse+6 (or +8 if bombs ≥ 3). See `docs/LEVEL_PASS.md`.  
5. **Board size:** softMax caps in `boardView.relayout` remain; prefer **shaped cavern layouts** over full solid 7×7/8×7 slabs.  
6. **Cavern identity:** layouts should read as chambers, corridors, twin galleries — not only open rectangles.  
7. **Stars:** win always ≥1★; 2★/3★ from score + leftover moves (existing `starsFromScore`).

### Movement pressure targets (legal-hint bot guidance)

| Band | Target win rate (greedy legal-hint) | Notes |
|------|-------------------------------------|--------|
| 151–200 | 15–40% | Act I-C open — readable but not free |
| 201–250 | 10–30% | Dual threats standard |
| 251–300 | 5–25% | Finale density; bomb-aware humans clear more |
| 301–400 | 10–30% | Act II open (slight reset of “new region” feel) |
| 401–500 | 5–20% | Peak multi-obj |
| 501–600 | 5–20% | Finale; optional “legend” bosses at 550/600 |

Bot loses bomb-heavy levels often — use **progress gates**, not 50% win rate, for defuse bands.

### Mechanic introduction (Act I-C / Act II)

Prefer **remixing existing systems** (crust layers, shadow, bombs, collect, conveyor, specials). New engine features only if a story beat *needs* a new toy:

| Optional later systems (not launch blockers) | Earliest band |
|----------------------------------------------|---------------|
| Conveyor speed variants / dual-row tease | 201+ |
| Shadow layer 2 more common | 220+ |
| “Pinned” stones density spikes | 240+ |
| **Locking crust** (encased gem; swap blocked until chipped) | **~361–400 first teach; standard 401+** |
| True new blocker type (beyond crust lock) | Act II only, if at all |

### Crust / ice escalation (tile frost → lock)

**Current ship rule (Act I):** crust is **tile frost** — ice lives on the cell, gems still swap, adjacent matches chip layers, thick crust blocks clear-through on that cell. Fantasy copy must match: *“Frost on the tile — match nearby to chip it. Gems still move.”* Not “frozen gems can’t move.”

**Why delay lock:** Act I pressure should come from layout, multi-obj, fuse, and dual threats — not a new control contract on top of the launch spine. Locking is a **story-gated rule change**, not a launch fix.

| Band | Story beat | Ice rules | Design intent |
|------|------------|-----------|---------------|
| **1–150** | Arrival / classic mine–harbor | **Tile frost only** (movable) | Teach chip-adjacent; low punishment |
| **151–300** | Under-Crown / Outer Channels | Still **tile frost**; layers 2–3 more often | Act I-C pressure from layout + multi-obj |
| **301–360** | Fracture / Storm opens | Still movable frost; denser crust goals OK | Soft “new region” reset; no new swipe rules |
| **361–400** | Fracture *bites* | **First locking ice** — rare, 1-layer, teach-clear | New toy after map identity re-settles |
| **401–500** | Rival systems | Lock is a **normal tool** on bosses + dual threats | Peak density; lock + existing systems |
| **501–600** | Crown / Treaty finale | Lock + multi-layer common on bosses; keep some free frost for breath | Ceremony, not pure lock boards |
| **601+** | Act III / prestige | Full lock variants / combos only if 301–600 retention holds | Optional escalation |

**Safer teach path (optional):** first lock only on bosses **370 / 380 / 390**, then bulk use from **401**. Casual Act II-A clears never require the new rule.

**Visual contract (required when lock ships):** two readable states — *rimed tile* (can still swap) vs *sealed / encased* (must chip first). Same art family, distinct silhouette or rim so players never guess.

**Board rule:** never freeze the entire playable area as lock forever; leave free corridors so the game still reads as match-3.

**Story lines (theme packs):**

| Product | Movable frost (Act I) | Locking seal (Act II mid+) |
|---------|----------------------|----------------------------|
| **Crystalline** | Floor rime / cold stone | “The mountain holds the gem” |
| **Lantern Harbor** | Deck frost / brine glaze | “Tide seals the buoy” |

## 5. Meta dual-loop alignment (future-proof sinks)

Level volume without meta sinks creates **currency pile-up**.

| Meta pack | Timing | Purpose |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Stages 1–4 | Launch spine (L1–150) | Classic Mouth → Deep Geode / Pier → Festival |
| **Stages 5–6** | Act I-C open (L151–220) | Under-Crown / Outer Channel |
| **Stages 7–8** | Act I-C close (L211–300) | Black Heartwalk → Seal / Quiet Slip → Channel Crown |
| Stages 9+ | **Act II (301+)** later | Phase 2 place goals (not shipped yet) |
| Prestige recolors | Optional | Low art cost if stages lag |

**Rule:** Do not reprice owned stage 1–4 props in live saves. New content only.

Companion lines should get **Act I-C** and **Act II** beat packs (title / win / lose / cavern) when those bands ship — not one eternal set of 5 lines.
