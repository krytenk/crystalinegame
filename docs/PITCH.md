## Problem / opportunity

Traditional match-3 is consolidated. Growing titles blend **puzzle skill**, **collection**, **hybrid events**, **cozy idle meta**, and **ease-of-play** monetization instead of pure appointment dark patterns. Crystalline is a **working map** of those systems in original crystal-mine IP.

## Product pillars

1. **Core match-3** — cascade resolve, power crystals, Living Core, conveyor rows (11+), soft auto-hints  
2. **Crystal Cavern** — essence → furnish staged chambers with placement ceremony  
3. **Live-ops layer** — endless album, Mine Rush hybrid event, daily dive goal, win streak  
4. **Cozy idle** — offline essence drip from furnishings (claim anytime)  
5. **Ethical shop (simulated)** — 7d/30d ad passes, Comfort Tools, no real charges
